Once upon a time, there lived a small family.The family consists of a father,a mother,and seven children- one boy and six girls.The father was a farmer and the mother was a housewife. The elder daughter was very mature,and the second daughter was very talented. Her only aim in life was to became an educated person,she really wanted to study above 8 th standard. All their daughters and their son studied until 10 th standard, but the father could not afford education for all of them and he doesn't interested in women higher studies, but the second daughter was very determined to continue her studies and she did not want to get married, her only goal was to study.Her father enrolled her son in engineering college after completing 12th standard but stopped second daughter from studying. After fighting with her father, she went to service home in the city and other daughter went with their aunt (mother's sister) to study in the city. She continued her schooling and then earned a degree in college.After completing college,she worked in lab,dress shops,etc for to survive on her own. Meanwhile,her elder sister got married at a young age to her maternal uncle. Then the family forced the second daughter to get married as well,but she did not like that.Due to family pressure, she was forced to get married. After marriage,she got a government job in a school.she was very happy to get that job,in that time she was pregnant and she doesn't bother about that and continued her job until 8 months of pregnant.Life goes smoothly for her and They got a baby girl first baby of their family because elder daughter doesnt have any children so the family was very happy to have a baby girl. After two months of delivery suddenly one day her husband had a heart attack and died while came to her home to see his wife and baby girl. Her life became tragic again, but she did'nt let her sadness bury her.she rose a like a phoenix from the ashes and continued her job and raised her daughter with great care and love with her family support.She gave her daughter a good education and her daughter became an IAS officer and she was very proud of her daughter and her daughter was also very proud of her mother.They both living happily together with their family Moral of the story: Never give up on your dreams, no matter what obstacles you face. With determination and hard work, you can achieve anything you set your mind to.
